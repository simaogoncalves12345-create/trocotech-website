About TrocoTech

A technology company built on curiosity, purpose and a long-term vision.

TrocoTech is a technology company dedicated to developing applications, artificial intelligence, digital platforms and technological solutions.

The company began in Angola and carries a vision of international growth, aiming to build technology that is useful, accessible and made with care — from the first idea to the final product.

Mission

Create technology that is useful, accessible and innovative.

Vision

Build a technology company with international reach, starting in Angola.

Values

Innovation, simplicity, security, creativity and purpose.

Our Story

TrocoTech started as an idea: to build technology that solves real problems in a simple and honest way. From Angola, the company began developing applications and platforms, always with a focus on quality, clarity and purpose.

Today, TrocoTech continues to explore new areas — from financial technology to artificial intelligence — always with the same commitment: to create tools that make a difference.

How We Work

Think

We understand the problem before writing a single line of code.

Design

We design simple, elegant and human-centered experiences.

Build

We develop with focus on performance, security and clarity.

Evolve

We improve continuously, based on real usage and real feedback.

Principles

  • Technology should be useful before it is impressive.
  • Simplicity is a feature, not a limitation.
  • Security and privacy are not optional.
  • Every product must have a clear purpose.
  • Long-term thinking over short-term noise.
